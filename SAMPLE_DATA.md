## How to Use

1. **Open the app** — Go to `http://localhost:3000`
2. **Click "Edit Values"** tab
3. **Scroll to bottom** — Find "Import CSV" button
4. **Select this file** — `sample-data.csv`
5. **Confirm import** — Click "Import" when prompted
6. **See the data** — Dashboard now shows sample data with Budget vs. Actual calculations

## What to Look For

Once imported, check out:

✅ **Budget Allocation Bar** — Shows 50% Needs, 30% Wants, 20% Savings distribution  
✅ **Budget vs. Actual Dashboard** — Compare budgeted vs. actual spending:
   - **Needs**: $1,950 budgeted vs. $1,870 actual (96% — On Track ✓)
   - **Wants**: $1,170 budgeted vs. $1,063 actual (91% — On Track ✓)
   - **Savings**: $780 budgeted vs. $867 actual (111% — Over, but good!)

✅ **Spending Analytics** — View historical spending by category  
✅ **Wants Tracker** — Current bi-weekly wants budget with progress  
✅ **Income Streams** — Three income sources with monthly totals  
✅ **Expense Cards** — Fixed monthly expenses broken down by category  
✅ **Credit Cards** — View balances against limits  

## Next Steps

After exploring with sample data:

1. **Clear it** — Re-import or manually clear to start fresh with your own data
2. **Add your data** — Enter your real income streams, expenses, and spending
3. **Track spending** — Use the Wants Tracker for bi-weekly spending envelope
4. **Monitor budget** — Check the Budget vs. Actual Dashboard monthly

## Need to Reset?

To return to a blank slate:
1. Go to **Edit Values** tab
2. Scroll down and look for a "Reset" or "Clear All Data" option, OR
3. Open browser DevTools (F12) → Application → Local Storage → Clear `penny_state_v2`
