Needs Budget
What are Needs?
Fixed, essential expenses you must pay every month — rent, utilities, groceries, transportation, and insurance. Every expense card you create counts toward this budget.
Total Income × Needs %
e.g. $3,900 × 50% = $1,950 / mo

Wants Budget
What are Wants?
Discretionary spending on things you enjoy but don't strictly need — dining out, entertainment, subscriptions, and shopping. Tracked via the bi-weekly Wants Envelope, which resets every two weeks.
Total Income × Wants %
e.g. $3,900 × 30% = $1,170 / mo
→ $585 per bi-weekly envelope

Savings Budget
What is Savings?
Money set aside each month for future goals, emergencies, and investments. Distribute this amount across your savings accounts — any unallocated remainder is shown as a warning.
Total Income × Savings %
e.g. $3,900 × 20% = $780 / mo
→ $390 per bi-weekly period
