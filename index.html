            User Guide

            A Penny For Our Thoughts is a personal finance dashboard built on the 50/30/20 budget rule. Everything runs in your browser — no account, no server, no internet required after the first load.

            In this guide Income Streams Budget Allocation Wants Tracker Expense Cards Subscriptions Savings & Goals Loans & Credit Cards Wishlist Net Worth Expense Schedule Transaction Rules Import & Export

            💰 Income Streams

            Add every source of income under Income Overview. Each stream has a name, a monthly dollar amount, and an optional bi-weekly toggle for pay cheques received every two weeks.

            • Click + Add Stream to create a new income source.
            • Enable Bi-wk for pay cheques — the app will track spending against 2-week pay periods instead of calendar months.
            • The Total Monthly Income card updates instantly and drives all budget calculations.

            📊 Budget Allocation

            The coloured bar below the income cards shows your 50/30/20 split. Click Edit to adjust the percentages — they must always sum to 100%.

            • Needs (purple) — rent, groceries, utilities, insurance.
            • Wants (teal) — dining, entertainment, hobbies.
            • Savings (amber) — emergency fund, investments, retirement.

            The Budget vs. Actual panel below the bar shows how your actual spending compares to each allocation this month, with green / amber / red status indicators.

            🛍 Wants Tracker

            Track discretionary spending against your Wants envelope. Each entry is a purchase with a name, amount, and category.

            • The donut chart shows spending by category for the current pay period.
            • Use the Spending History section to browse and filter past periods.
            • The envelope resets each bi-weekly period based on your pay anchor date (set under Pay Period in the Wants section).

            💳 Expense Cards

            Model your fixed monthly bills by payment method. Each card (e.g. Visa, bank account) holds a list of expense items.

            • Click + Add Card to create a new payment method.
            • Add items with a name and monthly amount. Enable Bi-wk for expenses charged every two weeks — the app doubles the amount for monthly comparison.
            • Set an optional Due Day (1–31) so items appear in the Expense Schedule.
            • The card's TOTAL includes any linked subscription charges due this month.
            • Subscriptions appear as read-only ↻ rows — edit them from the Subscriptions section.

            🔄 Subscriptions

            Track recurring services (streaming, SaaS, utilities). Every subscription must be linked to a payment card so it integrates into your budget automatically.

            • Frequencies: Monthly, Quarterly, Bi-Yearly (every 6 months), Annual.
            • Non-monthly subscriptions appear on the linked card only in the month they renew. Other months show a greyed-out "Next: [date]" row.
            • The ⚠ No card chip flags subscriptions imported from older data — edit them to link a card.
            • A duplicate warning appears in the modal if the subscription name matches an existing item on the chosen card.
            • Set Budget Type to Needs or Wants so the renewal counts against the correct category.

            🏦 Savings & Goals

            Track savings account balances and set targets for each account.

            • Each account has a Balance and a Default Monthly Allocation.
            • Click Add Goal to set a target amount and target month/year for an account.
            • The goal card shows progress %, monthly savings needed, and an on-track / caution / off-track status.

            🏛 Loans & Credit Cards

            Track outstanding debt balances for visibility in your net worth calculation.

            • Loans: Enter remaining balance and original amount to see payoff progress.
            • Credit Cards: Enter current balance and credit limit to track utilisation.
            • Both feed into the Liabilities side of the Net Worth tracker.

            🎯 Wishlist

            A simple list of items you want to save up for. Add an icon, name, and optional URL. No budget calculations — purely a reference list to keep you motivated.

            📈 Net Worth

            Your net worth is calculated automatically each month from your assets and liabilities.

            • Assets: Add savings balances, investments, property, vehicles, and other valuables.
            • Liabilities: Loan balances and credit card balances are pulled in automatically.
            • The chart shows up to 12 months of history — a snapshot is recorded once per calendar month on page load.

            📅 Expense Schedule

            The Schedule tab shows a 3-month calendar view of all bills with due dates, sorted by day. Subscription renewals appear automatically. Regular expense items appear when you set a Due Day on the item.

            ⚙️ Transaction Rules

            Rules auto-categorise wants purchases as you add them. Each rule matches against the purchase name using one of three modes:

            • Contains — name includes the pattern anywhere (e.g. "coffee" → matches "Tim Hortons coffee")
            • Starts with — name begins with the pattern.
            • Exact — name matches exactly.

            Rules are tested in order — first match wins. Manage them via the Rules section on the Dashboard.

            📤 Import & Export

            All data can be exported to a single CSV file and re-imported later. Use this to back up your data, migrate between browsers, or edit values in a spreadsheet.

            • Click Export in the header to download penny-export.csv.
            • Click Import and select a .csv file to restore. This replaces all current data.
            • See the for the full file format specification.

            FAQ & Troubleshooting

            Answers to the most common questions.

            My data disappeared after clearing the browser / using incognito mode — why?

            All data is stored in your browser's localStorage. Clearing your browser's site data, or using a private / incognito window, wipes that storage. To avoid data loss, export a CSV backup regularly — click Export in the header. You can re-import it at any time.

            How do I back up my data?

            Click Export in the top-right header. A file called penny-export.csv will download to your device. Store it somewhere safe (cloud drive, email to yourself). To restore, click Import and select the file — note that importing replaces all current data.

            Can I use this on multiple devices (phone + laptop)?

            Not automatically — there is no sync or cloud storage. To move data between devices, export on one device and import on the other. For regular use on a single device, the browser's localStorage persists across sessions as long as you don't clear site data.

            What's the difference between Needs and Wants?

            Needs are essential fixed expenses you must pay — rent, mortgage, utilities, groceries, insurance, minimum loan payments. Wants are discretionary spending you choose — dining out, entertainment, subscriptions to non-essential services, hobbies. If you could survive without it, it's a Want.

            My subscription shows "⚠ No card" — what do I do?

            This chip appears on subscriptions that were saved before the card-linking feature was added. Click the ✎ Edit button on the subscription, select the payment card it's charged to, and save. The chip will be replaced with the card name and the subscription will appear on that card's expense list.

            Why isn't my quarterly / annual subscription showing on my card this month?

            Non-monthly subscriptions only appear as active (and count toward the budget) in the month they renew. In other months they appear as a greyed-out "Next: [date]" row. The renewal date is calculated from the Next Renewal Date you set in the subscription modal — make sure it's set to the correct upcoming date.

            How do I change the 50/30/20 split?

            Click the Edit button in the Budget Allocation bar on the Dashboard. Enter your preferred percentages — they must total exactly 100%. Click Save to apply.

            My CSV import failed — what went wrong?

            Common causes: (1) The file was not exported from this app — only files in the Penny CSV format are supported. (2) The file was manually edited and a column was added, removed, or renamed. (3) A value contains a comma that wasn't quoted properly. Try re-exporting a fresh backup and importing that to verify the format is correct. See the for the expected structure.

            The Budget vs. Actual numbers don't look right — how is "actual" calculated?

            For Needs: the sum of all expense card items plus any Needs subscriptions that renewed this calendar month. For Wants: all purchases in the current bi-weekly period plus any Wants subscriptions that renewed during that period. For Savings: the total of default monthly allocations across all savings accounts.

            How much data can I store?

            localStorage is typically limited to 5 MB per origin by browsers. A few years of spending history and dozens of expenses will be well within this limit. If you ever hit the limit, exporting and clearing old spending history is the recommended workaround.

            Privacy & Data

            Your financial data never leaves your device. Here's exactly how it works.

            🔒
            100% local storage

            All data is saved in your browser's localStorage under the key penny_state_v2. Nothing is transmitted to any server, ever. The app works entirely offline after the initial page load.

            📤
            Backups via CSV export

            The only way data leaves your device is when you click Export. The downloaded penny-export.csv file goes to your device's downloads folder — it is not uploaded anywhere. Treat it like any sensitive financial document.

            🌐
            No tracking, no analytics, no cookies

            The app sets no cookies and loads no third-party analytics scripts. The only external resource is the Chart.js library (loaded from Cloudflare CDN for charting). No usage data is collected.

            ⚠️
            Shared computers & incognito mode

            If you use a shared computer, other users of the same browser profile can access your data via localStorage. Use a separate browser profile or private browsing. Note: private / incognito windows do not persist localStorage between sessions — your data will be lost when you close the window.

            🗑
            Clearing your data

            Click Clear in the header to wipe all app data permanently. This action cannot be undone — export a backup first. You can also clear the data manually in browser DevTools under Application → Storage → localStorage.

            💾
            Storage limit

            Most browsers allow up to 5 MB of localStorage per origin. A typical year of full data usage is well under 1 MB. If you ever hit the limit, your browser will silently fail to save new data — export and clear old spending history to free up space.

            CSV Format Reference

            The export file is a plain-text CSV divided into named sections. Each section begins with a SECTION:name header row, followed by a column-name row, then data rows. Sections are separated by a blank line.

            Tip: The safest way to create an import file is to export first, edit the CSV in a spreadsheet app, then re-import. Manually constructed files must follow this format exactly — extra columns or renamed headers will cause the section to be silently skipped.
