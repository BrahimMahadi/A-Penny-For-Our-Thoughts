Lab 3 · scroll chaining

overscroll-behavior

Scroll the violet inner box to its bottom, then keep scrolling. On the left the scroll chains to the page behind it — that is what makes a modal drag the whole dashboard underneath it, and what lets a downward pull trigger the browser's pull-to-refresh mid-form.
